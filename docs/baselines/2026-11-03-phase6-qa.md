## Bugs found and fixed (v0.7.3)
1. **No rollback history after a re-point.**
   - **Cause:** `repair --marketplace <new>` removes the marketplace, which makes the real CLI uninstall its plugins. The "before" snapshot was taken after that, so it saw nothing installed and recorded no history. The recorded source was also the new one, not the old one.
   - **Fix:** take the snapshot and the previous source before the marketplace is touched.
   - **Test fake:** the fake `claude` now cascades the uninstall the way the real CLI does.
   - **Regression test:** `update by re-pointing the marketplace records history…`. It fails without the fix.
2. **Debug classifier miss.**
   - **Cause:** "The cart total **is wrong** … **Investigate why**" was classified `no-match`, so no contract was added and no skill was activated.
   - **Fix:** the debug rule now also matches `is/are/was/looks/comes out wrong|incorrect`, `incorrect(ly)`, and `investigate why/what/how/the bug|issue|problem|failure`.
   - **Tests:** cases added, including non-debug near-misses ("rejects the wrong input type" → feature). The full suite's classes are unchanged.
